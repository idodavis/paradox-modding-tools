# Patch Notes

The patch notes of the workspace's game, read from its community wiki.

{{< shot caption="Patch Notes: the versions, a note, and its contents." >}}

**Versions**, on the left, lists the game's versions, newest first, each followed by its hotfixes and with its code
name where the note gives one ("Crozier"); the note's header gives its release date. **Filter
versions** narrows the list. The newest is marked **Latest** and opens first; a dot marks a note with a modding
section.

The note opens in the middle: **Ctrl F** finds in it (**Enter** and **Shift Enter** for the next and previous
match), links to its own headings jump there, and **Open on the wiki** opens the page in your browser. **On this
page** lists its headings and marks the one in view; drag its edge to resize it, and its button hides it to give the
note more room.

PMT fetches the notes at every start, in the background, for each game you have an install of: only the pages that
changed, and a page that fails keeps its last copy. Then they read without a connection. The status bar says when
they were last cached. The [IDE's Wiki pane]({{< relref "/pages/ide#wiki" >}}) reads the same wiki.
